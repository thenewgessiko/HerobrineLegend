# HerobrineLegend 0.4.3 — Audio by Fear phase

- Fear 0–29: successful ambient sound events alternate between eerie sounds and creature sounds.
- Fear 30–99: appearance plays only a non-entity eerie sound; after 2 seconds the controller plays ENTITY_ENDERMAN_SCREAM if Herobrine is still stalking.
- Fear 100 hunting: appearance plays only a non-entity eerie sound; no delayed Enderman scream.
- ENTITY_ENDERMAN_STARE remains tied to eye-triggered vanish/jumpscare, never the random pool.
- 20-second cooldowns, ritual Fear reset, 6 hunts and jumping are unchanged.
- New config pools: soundscape.low-fear.entity-events and soundscape.appearance.eerie-events.
